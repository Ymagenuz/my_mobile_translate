## 0.1.0 — not released yet

- Translate, look up words and back-translate through your own API key, with GPTSAPI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible provider. Results stream in as Markdown, with **Stop**, **Try again** and history.
- Translate from any app's text-selection menu, from the share sheet, or from the clipboard with a Quick Settings tile.
- **Select on screen**: scan whatever is on the screen and pick words with text, plus, minus, lasso, paragraph and area pens.
- Open photos and screenshots with the same pens, or read a whole image with the vision model.
- English and 简体中文 interface.
